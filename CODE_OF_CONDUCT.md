# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in DepRisk, whether through code, issues, discussions, or reviews, to be a respectful and productive experience for everyone, regardless of background, nationality, language, gender identity or expression, age, disability, ethnicity, religion, or level of experience.

This project adopts the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) by reference. The summary below states how we apply them here.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and professional. Assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback; critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be honest about uncertainty and mistakes, including your own. This is a security and correctness project, and candor about limits is a core value.
- Respect that contributors work in different languages and time zones; be patient with language differences.
- Credit others' work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language or jokes
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberate derailing of discussions
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors (for example, demanding fixes or merges)
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and to public spaces when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

To report a violation, contact the maintainers privately. Use the email address on the maintainer's public GitHub profile, or the repository's **Security** tab (private report) if you cannot reach us otherwise. Reports are handled confidentially, and we will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

Consequences are proportionate and may include, in order: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban.
